Final Project Presentation

Workplace Zakat
Platform

Bridging Web2 Corporate HR systems with Web3 Cryptographic Verification for secure, auditable Zakat payroll deductions.

1. The Problem

The Corporate "Trust Deficit"

No Automation

Employees handle complex Zakat calculations manually. HR systems lack native religious giving modules.

Blind Trust

If HR does handle deductions, employees must blindly trust internal, easily-altered databases. There is no mathematical proof of integrity.

2. The Solution

Immutable Audit Trails

A hybrid application where HR manages payroll in Web2, but finalizes records in Web3.

👤

Employee Experience

Clean, read-only receipt portal. No crypto knowledge required.

🛡️

HR Administration

Uses MetaMask to digitally sign and lock the payroll record on the blockchain.

3. Data Architecture

What goes on-chain?

Off-Chain (PostgreSQL)

  • • Employee Names
  • • Email Addresses
  • • Exact Salary / Deduction Amounts
  • Why? Privacy compliance & zero gas fees for heavy data.

On-Chain (Sepolia)

  • • Cryptographic Hash of the Payload
  • • Previous Block Hash (Chain Link)
  • • HR Admin's Digital Signature
  • Why? Mathematical proof the data was never altered.

5. Web3 Integration

MetaMask as a Digital Notary

In this application, no money moves on the blockchain. The actual fiat transfer happens via standard banking.

Why use MetaMask then?

MetaMask acts as the HR Admin's secure ID. It is used to authorize the payment of Sepolia network gas fees and to mathematically sign the transaction, proving that the official corporate wallet authorized the payroll log on that exact date.
